**Currently building [BhashaBlend](https://github.com/GagDrag/BhashaBlend)** — video in, multilingual subtitles out, aimed at Indian classrooms where the lecture is in one language and the student thinks in another. Gemini 2.5 Flash for transcription and translation, Celery + Valkey for the job queue, Postgres for state, Astro + Clerk on the front, WebSockets streaming progress back to the browser.

**Before that:** diffusion-model image editing, real-time chat infrastructure, and a measurement tool that people actually use.

**Now:** open to internships and collaboration on anything at the intersection of ML and accessibility.

**BhashaBlend** — the whole pipeline runs on one multimodal model. An earlier version ran WhisperX + FFmpeg on a GPU; replacing it with Gemini 2.5 Flash deleted the entire local ML stack and the hardware bill with it.

**[Real Online Ruler](https://realonlineruler.com)** — every other online ruler is an image that assumes your screen is the same as everyone else's. This one makes you calibrate first, so an inch is an inch. Astro, React, Tailwind v4.

**ThreadShift** — garment editing with Stable Diffusion and segmentation: change what someone is wearing without touching the rest of the frame.

**Discord-Clone-2** — real-time chat with servers, roles and auth, on Next.js with Stream and Clerk.

Also around: **OrganEase**, a MERN platform connecting organ donors and recipients.
